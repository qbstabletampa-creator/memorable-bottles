Before you begin

Pieces start at $399, and that includes shipping the finished piece back to you. From there you can add foil lettering ($10), a nameplate ($25), or step up to a larger frame (from $499). The only thing you cover is getting your bottle to me.

Honestly, just about anything with a story. Wine and champagne are the most common, but I've framed spirits, and even a baseball bat. If you saved it, there's a good chance I can turn it into something worth hanging. Not sure yours works? Send me a photo and I'll tell you.

Most people ship USPS or FedEx, nothing fancy. Just pack it well, plenty of bubble wrap or cushion so it arrives safe and whole. No one has ever sent me a bottle that showed up damaged, so you've got this. And if you can, save the cork and label too, they make the final piece feel complete.

Once your bottle reaches me, I ask for up to 21 days, though most pieces go out sooner. In a hurry? I offer a 10-day rush once it arrives for an extra $30.

A $100 deposit secures your order and reserves your spot. Once your piece is finished, I'll send an invoice for the balance before it ships. That's it, no surprises.
