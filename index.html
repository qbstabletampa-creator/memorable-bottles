Good Questions

Before you ask

Do you use my actual bottle?

Yes. That's the whole point. Your real bottle, from your real moment, becomes the art. Every label, every piece of glass is yours.

What if I already opened or saved it?

Perfect. Most pieces start with a bottle you've kept on a shelf since the big day. Once you reserve, we'll send you the studio address so you can ship it in.

How do I get my bottle to you?

You ship it to us however works best for you. Just pack it with plenty of cushion, bubble wrap, paper, whatever keeps it safe in transit. We'll send the address as soon as you reserve your spot.

I'm not in Tampa. Can you still make mine?

Absolutely. We're based in Tampa, FL and ship finished pieces anywhere in the US.

How long does it take?

About two weeks from the day your bottle arrives at the studio. Every piece is made by hand, so we take the time to get it right.

How much does it cost?

Pieces start at $399, and run to $499 depending on the size and any add ons you choose. Reach out with your moment and we'll help you pick the right size and put together your quote.

Do I get to see it before it's final?

Yes. You approve the design before it's finished, so the piece on your wall is exactly what you wanted.
